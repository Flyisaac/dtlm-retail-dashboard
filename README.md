# DTLM Retail — Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard built for DTLM Retail analysing five months of transactional sales data across 10 product categories, 10 sales teams, and 10 US states.

Built as part of the Dataleum Data Analytics certification programme.

## What I Built
- Calculated **revenue and profit** for every order in the dataset
- Built **5 analytical reports** covering revenue trends, top products, category contribution, sales team performance, and state-level distribution
- Designed a fully interactive **dashboard with slicers** filterable by Product Category and Date
- Extracted **3 key business insights** from the data

## Key Findings
- **Decoratives** was the top-performing category, generating $13.3M in revenue — 30% of total
- The **top 2 revenue products in January** were identified from the product breakdown
- **Store count** showed the strongest correlation with revenue per state across the top 10 states
- **Nicholas Cunningham** led the top 10 sales teams with $1.86M in revenue
